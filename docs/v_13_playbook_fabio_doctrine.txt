2. CORE PRINCIPLES
------------------------------------------------------------
Market operates in two states:
    a. BALANCE → Price rotates around fair value (70% of the time).
    b. IMBALANCE → Price moves aggressively to find new fair value.

Trade execution is authorized only when three align:
    1. Market State confirmed.
    2. Location identified via Volume Profile (LVN, POC).
    3. Aggression validated via Order Flow (CVD pressure, print size, imbalance).

If any of the above are missing → TRADE BLOCKED.

4. TRADE MODEL TRANSLATION
------------------------------------------------------------
MODEL 1: TREND CONTINUATION (Out-of-Balance → New Balance)
------------------------------------------------------------
Conditions:
    - Market out of balance, showing displacement + directional momentum.
    - Volume Profile applied to impulse leg → Identify LVNs.
    - Wait for retracement into LVN.

Entry Trigger:
    - Aggression confirmation in trend direction (buy/sell footprint dominance).
    - Order Flow Delta supports directional continuation.

Stop Loss:
    - 1–2 tick buffer below/above aggression print.

Risk Size:
    - 0.25–0.5% capital allocation per mission.

Target:
    - Previous balance Point of Control (POC).
    - Optional trail if strong displacement persists.

Operational Notes:
    - Works best in New York Session (NASDAQ / ES futures sim).
    - Avoid early London fakeouts.
    - Skip if CVD pressure not confirmed.

MODEL 2: MEAN REVERSION (Failed Breakout → Return to Balance)
------------------------------------------------------------
Conditions:
    - Market in balance → failed breakout attempt.
    - Price reclaims prior value → confirm re-entry into range.
    - Volume Profile on reclaim leg → LVNs become snap-back zones.

Entry Trigger:
    - Aggression reversal confirmed (buyers/sellers losing control).
    - Enter toward balance POC.

Stop Loss:
    - 1–2 tick buffer beyond aggression print.
    - No widening allowed; immediate invalidation = exit.

Target:
    - Center of Value (POC).
    - Avoid stretching to opposite range extremes.

Operational Notes:
    - Works best in London session or low-volatility conditions.
    - Skip during compressed or choppy data.
