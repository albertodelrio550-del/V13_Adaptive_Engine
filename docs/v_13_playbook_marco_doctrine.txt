2. CORE PRINCIPLES
------------------------------------------------------------
The market seeks liquidity. Price moves to trigger stops (external liquidity) and reverses once trapped participants are caught.

Trade entry conditions:
    1. Liquidity identified (equal highs/lows or respected structure).
    2. Price sweeps liquidity and returns inside range.
    3. Trap confirmation through internal structure failure or false breakout.
    4. Directional bias established post-liquidity event.

Execution occurs **after** liquidity is taken, never before.

4. TRADE MODEL TRANSLATION
------------------------------------------------------------
MODEL 1: SELL TRAP (Liquidity Above High → Short Reversal)
------------------------------------------------------------
Conditions:
    - Identify prior high respected and caused move away.
    - Wait for price to trade above that high (liquidity taken).
    - Confirm internal structure shift (false breakout reaction).

Entry Trigger:
    - Enter short only **after** rejection candle closes back below liquidity level.
    - Confirm volume reduction and failed continuation attempt.

Stop Loss:
    - Place above the high that was swept.
    - 1–2 tick buffer to absorb residual volatility.

Target:
    - Equal lows or prior liquidity pocket below.

Operational Notes:
    - Execute during high-liquidity sessions (New York Open preferred).
    - Skip trades outside primary time window.
    - Avoid early entries before liquidity run completes.

MODEL 2: BUY TRAP (Liquidity Below Low → Long Reversal)
------------------------------------------------------------
Conditions:
    - Identify low that caused a move away (created liquidity below).
    - Wait for price to run below that low (stop sweep event).
    - Confirm failure to extend lower → internal structure reclaim.

Entry Trigger:
    - Enter long once price reclaims above swept level.
    - Confirmation: reduction in sell aggression or shift in footprint delta.

Stop Loss:
    - Below the low just taken.
    - Small 1–2 tick buffer for volatility.

Target:
    - Liquidity resting at highs or value edge above.

Operational Notes:
    - Use primarily during London or New York open.
    - Avoid setups formed during low-volume hours.
