------------------------------------------------------------
2. CORE PRINCIPLES
------------------------------------------------------------
Phase Logic:
    • **Accumulation** — Market builds liquidity through range-bound chop. Low conviction, low probability.
    • **Manipulation** — Stop runs or false breakouts occur. Emotional traps form.
    • **Distribution** — Real move confirms, strong directional intent, and clean displacement.

Doctrine Objective:
    Trade **only** when Distribution is confirmed.
    Stay **flat** during Accumulation.
    **Observe** Manipulation but never engage inside it.

4. PHASE TRANSLATION
------------------------------------------------------------
ACCUMULATION → Manipulation → Distribution (AMD Cycle)
------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 1: **Accumulation**
    - Sideways range, low volatility, weak conviction.
    - Objective: Capital preservation.
    - Behavior: System disables scalp entries.
    - Action: Observe and map liquidity build-up.

Phase 2: **Manipulation**
    - Sudden liquidity sweeps (above/below range).
    - Objective: Confirmation-only monitoring.
    - Behavior: Alert Commander of potential trap formation.
    - Action: Wait for displacement confirmation — no entries.

Phase 3: **Distribution**
    - Confirmed break of range, displacement candle + fair value gap.
    - Objective: Execute with conviction.
    - Behavior: Activate primary trade models (Fabio & Marco Doctrines).
    - Action: Deploy trades aligned with new trend direction.
